02 / EXPERIMENT INTEGRITY

Fixed rules, separated periods

70 / 30

The 5-bar / 20-bar crossover rule is frozen. The first 70% of rows form the development window; the final 30% form a later holdout. Each window starts with an independent virtual balance, while the holdout may inspect earlier completed bars for indicator warmup.

WHAT THE REPORT DISTINGUISHES

Observed imported OHLCV versus derived indicators; training versus holdout; simulated fills versus actual executions; declared provenance versus independently verified evidence.

MODEL ASSUMPTIONS

Next-session open, fixed adverse slippage, modeled transaction fees, initial paper allocation limits. The experiment does not model order-book impact, full market liquidity, splits or dividend-adjusted returns.
